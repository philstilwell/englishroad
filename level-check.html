Find your English level and what to practice.

Answer English questions from a bank of 4,200 items. The items appear in a largely random order, with the first questions being generally easier. The app estimates your level for TOEFL, IELTS, TOEIC, and CEFR. It also shows grammar and vocabulary areas that need work.

  1. Choose one answer.
  2. See your first level after 5 answers.
  3. Your answers are saved in this browser if the page refreshes.
  4. After 100 questions, you get a short unofficial report. It shows your level range, test score estimates, correct answers, strong areas, things to practice, and the date. You can copy the report text or take a screenshot.
