    How this English grammar check helps

    What grammar and vocabulary will I review?

    Questions draw on 35 topics, including verb tenses, articles, prepositions, sentence structure, everyday words, and workplace vocabulary. Later questions respond to your earlier answers while keeping a variety of topics. You can also choose a specific grammar or vocabulary topic.

    What happens after 25 questions?

    You receive a report of your answers and suggestions for further practice. You can stop there or continue to 50 questions for more evidence within this activity. The report is a study aid, not an official English level or examination result. Read how practice suggestions are calculated.

    How can I practice the questions I found difficult?

    Use the report to open a focused quiz, choose a targeted follow-up set, or copy a complete AI tutoring prompt. The prompt includes the selected questions and your answers, so you can ask for a grammar explanation, vocabulary practice, or a dialogue without writing a prompt yourself.

    Privacy and use

    No login is required. Your quiz progress can be saved in this browser so a refresh can continue the quiz. Cloudflare Web Analytics helps us understand traffic. This is an activity report. Method, limitations, privacy, and deleting saved data.
