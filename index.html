Questions about English Road

Are these English quizzes free?

Yes. The grammar and vocabulary check, practice quizzes, answer explanations, and copy-ready AI prompts are free to use without an English Road account. If you use a separate AI service, its own access rules and pricing apply.

Which practice band should I choose?

Choose A1 for beginner material, A2 for elementary, B1 for intermediate, B2 for upper intermediate, C1 for advanced, or C2 for very advanced material. Change bands if the questions feel too easy or too difficult. The grammar and vocabulary check can suggest a starting point.

Does the check give an official English level or certificate?

No. It reviews grammar and vocabulary and suggests further practice. It does not measure speaking, listening, extended writing, or overall proficiency, predict an examination score, or provide a certificate. Read how the results and practice bands work.

How is English Road different from English Ladder?

English Road focuses on interactive grammar and vocabulary quizzes. Our sister site, English Ladder, offers reading lessons, grammar material, and English for work. Use the two sites together to practice and then apply what you learn.
