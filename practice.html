How the scales compare

CEFR describes language ability. TOEIC and TOEFL are tests linked to those levels. These are reference comparisons, not predicted scores from your English Road quiz.

CEFR levels and ETS score guidance
CEFRTOEIC Listening + ReadingTOEFL iBT Current 1–6 scale

A2225+L 110 · R 1152–2.5≈ 24–43 / 120
B1550+L 275 · R 2753–3.5≈ 44–71 / 120
B2785+L 400 · R 3854–4.5≈ 72–94 / 120
C1945+L 490 · R 4555–5.5≈ 95–113 / 120
C2Not mapped6≈ 114–120 / 120

TOEIC: totals out of 990 sum ETS’s recommended listening (L) and reading (R) minimums. Both section scores matter; a total alone can hide a weaker skill. These are guidelines. Speaking and Writing use separate scales; this mapping does not establish C2.

TOEFL: the 1–6 scale applies from January 21, 2026. The smaller figures combine ETS’s comparable 0–120 ranges; they are not the historical CEFR cut-offs. Overall scores can hide differences between skills.

A1 · Articles: “a”, “an”, and “the”

You are introducing your job. Choose the grammatically correct sentence.

Which sentence is correct?

  1. I am nurse.
  2. I am an nurse.
  3. I am a nurses.
  4. I am a nurse.
Show answer and explanation

Correct answer: I am a nurse.

Use 'a' before the singular job noun 'nurse': 'I am a nurse'. 'Nurse' begins with the consonant sound /n/.

A2 · Talking about future plans

Choose the reply that expresses Carlos's existing plan for next year.

Carlos has decided to take a course. What does he say?

  1. I'm going to study design.
  2. I studied design last year.
  3. I've studied design before.
  4. I study design every evening.
Show answer and explanation

Correct answer: I'm going to study design.

'Going to' expresses an intention Carlos has already formed for the future.
