### 1. Existential there introducing a singular place

ID: `coverage-advanced-sentence-structure-a1-241`

Tell a visitor about a place near the hotel.

Choose the grammatically correct sentence in standard written English.

1. There a pharmacy opposite the hotel.
2. There has a pharmacy opposite the hotel.
3. There is pharmacy a opposite the hotel.
4. There is a pharmacy opposite the hotel. **[Correct]**

**Explanation:** Use there is to introduce the existence of one place or thing.

**Choice feedback:**

- **There a pharmacy opposite the hotel.:** There needs a verb here: there is a pharmacy.
- **There has a pharmacy opposite the hotel.:** Existential there uses be, not has, to introduce the pharmacy.
- **There is pharmacy a opposite the hotel.:** The article goes before the noun: a pharmacy.
- **There is a pharmacy opposite the hotel.:** Use there is to introduce the existence of one place or thing.

**Decision:** replaced.

**Original findings:** Desk-report frame began twenty near-identical existential items with generic wrong-order feedback.

**Learning value:** Builds there + be + noun phrase and explains separate missing-verb and noun-order errors.

**Level rationale:** A concrete place and a short existence statement suit A1 foundations.

**Similarity review:** One genuine existential item retained in function, with the repeated desk series removed.
