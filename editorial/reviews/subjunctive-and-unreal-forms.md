### 1. Polite would-like request distinguished from an existing state

ID: `coverage-subjunctive-and-unreal-forms-a1-3601`

What does the traveler mean?

2. I want a quiet seat. **[Correct]**

**Explanation:** Would like is a polite way to express this wish or request.

**Choice feedback:**

- **I already have a quiet seat.:** I'd like expresses what the traveler wants, not what the traveler already has.
- **I want a quiet seat.:** Would like is a polite way to express this wish or request.
- **I usually sit in quiet places.:** The request concerns a seat now, not a usual habit.
- **I do not want to sit down.:** The traveler asks for a seat rather than refusing to sit.

**Decision:** replaced.

**Original findings:** Original Daniel/Mina wish-were item was too advanced for A1 and wrongly described were as a base-form request.

**Learning value:** Introduces a desired situation without treating the desire as already fulfilled.

**Level rationale:** A frequent travel request is an appropriate A1 foundation for later unreal language.

**Similarity review:** A request for a seat, not a formal subjunctive or a named-person wish-were frame.
