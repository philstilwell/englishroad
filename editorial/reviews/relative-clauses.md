### 1. Who as the subject of a person description

ID: `coverage-relative-clauses-a1-3121`

Choose the word that describes the person doing the repair.

The mechanic ___ repairs my car is very helpful.

1. who **[Correct]**
2. where
3. which
4. whose

**Explanation:** 'Who' refers to the mechanic and is the subject of 'repairs'.

**Choice feedback:**

- **who:** 'Who' refers to the mechanic and is the subject of 'repairs'.
- **where:** 'Where' introduces a place, not the person doing the repair.
- **which:** 'Which' normally describes a thing, not a named type of person in this use.

**Original findings:** Teacher/library original repeated a five-frame set and used undiagnostic feedback.

**Learning value:** Connects a person with an action that identifies them.

**Level rationale:** A short description of a familiar worker provides A1 foundations.

**Similarity review:** A mechanic is identified by repairing the speaker's car.
