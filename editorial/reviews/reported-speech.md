### 1. Speaker I becomes third-person subject in a present report

ID: `coverage-reported-speech-a1-3241`

Daniel says, 'I am ready.' Choose the report about Daniel, not about yourself.

Daniel says that ___ is ready.

1. I
2. him
3. he **[Correct]**
4. his

**Explanation:** 'He' refers to Daniel as the person who is ready.

**Choice feedback:**

- **I:** 'I' would refer to the person reporting the message, but Daniel is talking about himself.
- **him:** 'Him' is an object form; the person before 'is' needs a subject form.
- **he:** 'He' refers to Daniel as the person who is ready.
- **his:** 'His' shows possession and cannot stand as the subject meaning Daniel here.

**Decision:** replaced.

**Original findings:** Original Daniel/Mina tired item lacked source words and used nonsensical yesterday-today feedback targets.

**Learning value:** Establishes whose condition is being reported before introducing tense changes.

**Level rationale:** A short present statement and familiar subject pronouns fit A1 foundations.

**Similarity review:** A readiness message with no backshift or changing location.
