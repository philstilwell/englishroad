### 1. Lesson as a period of instruction

ID: `coverage-academic-vocabulary-a1-1`

My driving lesson is from two to three.

What does 'lesson' mean here?

1. a time when you repair something
2. a journey you make to work
3. a time when someone teaches you **[Correct]**
4. a break before you start work

**Explanation:** A lesson is a period of teaching, here learning to drive.

**Choice feedback:**

- **a time when you repair something:** Repair work fixes something; a driving lesson teaches a skill.
- **a journey you make to work:** A journey can involve driving, but 'lesson' names the teaching time.
- **a time when someone teaches you:** A lesson is a period of teaching, here learning to drive.
- **a break before you start work:** A lesson involves learning, not a rest from work.

**Decision:** replaced.

**Original findings:** Bare start-time sentence and mixed word-class definitions did not test lesson in a useful context.

**Learning value:** Recognizes teaching outside a school classroom.

**Level rationale:** A familiar adult skill and short time phrase suit A1.

**Similarity review:** Driving instruction, not class as a group or exam as assessment.
