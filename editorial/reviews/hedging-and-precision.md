### 1. Maybe for an uncertain location

ID: `coverage-hedging-and-precision-a1-1561`

You cannot find your phone. You think it is in your bag, but you are not sure.

Which sentence shows that you are not sure?

1. My phone is in my bag.
2. My phone was in my bag.
3. Maybe my phone is in my bag. **[Correct]**
4. My phone is not in my bag.

**Explanation:** 'Maybe' shows that the phone could be there, but you do not know.

**Choice feedback:**

- **My phone is in my bag.:** This states the place as a fact; you have not found the phone yet.
- **My phone was in my bag.:** 'Was' tells us about the past, not how sure you are now.
- **Maybe my phone is in my bag.:** 'Maybe' shows that the phone could be there, but you do not know.
- **My phone is not in my bag.:** This says the bag is not the place; you have not checked it.

**Decision:** replaced.

**Original findings:** Report frame lacks evidence; all wrong messages repeat an absolute-language claim.

**Learning value:** Mark a guess instead of stating a fact.

**Level rationale:** Short present-tense sentences with familiar belongings.

**Similarity review:** A missing phone motivates uncertainty about place.
