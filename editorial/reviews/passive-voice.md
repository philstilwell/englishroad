### 1. Understanding made in on a product label

ID: `coverage-passive-voice-a1-2281`

Choose the meaning of the label.

This jacket is made in Portugal.

1. People in Portugal make this jacket. **[Correct]**
2. People take this jacket to Portugal for cleaning.
3. This jacket is only worn in Portugal.
4. This jacket belongs to a person from Portugal.

**Explanation:** Is made in identifies the place of production: people make the jacket in Portugal.

**Choice feedback:**

- **People in Portugal make this jacket.:** Is made in identifies the place of production: people make the jacket in Portugal.
- **People take this jacket to Portugal for cleaning.:** The label concerns making the jacket, not cleaning it.
- **This jacket is only worn in Portugal.:** Made in gives its origin, not a rule about where to wear it.
- **This jacket belongs to a person from Portugal.:** The place of production does not identify the jacket's owner.

**Decision:** replaced.

**Original findings:** Original instruction described a document yesterday, while the sentence concerned a room today.

**Learning value:** Connects a common passive expression with product origin.

**Level rationale:** A familiar clothing label supports A1 recognition without transformation rules.

**Similarity review:** Product origin rather than a classroom cleaning routine.
