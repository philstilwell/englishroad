### 1. Concrete example after a general instruction

ID: `coverage-discourse-function-a1-1201`

A lunch note says, 'Bring a drink. For example, bring some water.'

What does the second sentence do?

1. It tells you to bring two drinks.
2. It names one possible drink. **[Correct]**
3. It explains why you need a drink.
4. It asks which drink you like.

**Explanation:** 'For example' introduces water as one drink you can bring.

**Choice feedback:**

- **It tells you to bring two drinks.:** Water is an example of a drink, not a second required drink.
- **It names one possible drink.:** 'For example' introduces water as one drink you can bring.
- **It explains why you need a drink.:** The sentence names a drink; it gives no reason for bringing one.
- **It asks which drink you like.:** The note offers an example and does not ask about your preference.

**Decision:** replaced.

**Original findings:** Bring-the-report example has a disconnected today prefix and identical wrong feedback.

**Learning value:** Recognize that an example is one possible member of a category.

**Level rationale:** Simple lunch language and a common example phrase.

**Similarity review:** Identifies an example's role rather than choosing a connecting word.
