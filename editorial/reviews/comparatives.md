### 1. Regular comparative with than

1. small
2. smaller **[Correct]**
3. smallest
4. more smaller

**Explanation:** Smaller compares the size of this bag with that one; than introduces the other bag.

**Choice feedback:**

- **small:** Small describes size but does not fit directly before than here.
