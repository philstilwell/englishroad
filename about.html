About English Road

Practice with a clear purpose.

English Road is maintained by Phil Stilwell. It offers free grammar and vocabulary practice alongside the reading lessons at English Ladder.

What your results mean

Level Check is a 100-question grammar and vocabulary review. Its report counts correct answers and shows results by topic. You see an explanation after each answer, so this is also a learning activity. Results from different attempts are not directly comparable.

It does not assess speaking, listening, extended writing, or overall English proficiency. It does not assign a measured Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) level, predict TOEFL, IELTS, or TOEIC scores, or provide a certificate. Earlier numerical confidence and examination predictions were removed in September 2026 because we did not have evidence to support them.

How questions are selected

The check starts with easier questions. A pacing rule uses all previous responses to guide the difficulty of later questions while keeping a variety of grammar and vocabulary topics. Incorrect answers cannot raise that difficulty cue. The cue is used only to choose questions; it is not a proficiency score.

How practice suggestions work

A1–C2 label approximate practice bands assigned to the content. After at least ten answers, we suggest the highest band in which you have answered at least five questions with at least 75% correct. If no band meets that rule, we suggest trying A1. This is a simple starting rule, not a validated placement threshold. Change bands whenever the material feels too easy or too difficult.

Topic suggestions show the number correct out of the number attempted. Fewer than three answers are labeled a small sample. A consistent area requires at least three attempts and 75% correct within this activity; that is not evidence of lasting mastery. Topic links open a focused quiz, which may contain fewer than 25 questions.

CEFR alignment requires evidence beyond assigning labels. See the Council of Europe’s examination-linking manual.

Questions and editorial review

The bank contains 659 distinct combinations of question text and answer choices, drawn from 4,200 generated variations. Both tools use the same bank and explanations. Identical combinations are removed, and questions do not repeat within an attempt.

Automated checks detect missing answers, duplicate choices, and known problem patterns. They cannot establish that every question has only one defensible answer. Editorial review is ongoing. The bank distinguishes draft material from items with a recorded assisted review; it has not been independently validated with learners or an assessment specialist.

If a question looks wrong, use its “Report a question problem” link. It opens a draft issue on GitHub, where you can describe the problem and submit it yourself. GitHub may require an account. Reports are public, so include the question identifier and explanation rather than personal details.

Privacy and saved progress

No English Road login is required. Questions are checked in your browser. Level Check and Practice each store one attempt in this browser when browser storage is available. Stored data includes the questions selected, answer choices, your responses, and progress; a completed Level Check also includes its completion time. The save status tells you whether the latest write succeeded.

This data is not automatically uploaded as a quiz report. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to resume it. Starting another attempt replaces the saved attempt for that tool. When a bank update makes an old attempt incompatible, we keep one previous-data backup when browser storage permits; it is available from the update notice and included in the download and deletion controls below. Use Download progress for a readable record, especially if browser storage is unavailable. Downloads are records, not files the quiz can import.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is used on the public pages to understand traffic. Hosting and analytics services receive ordinary page requests. English Road does not send your selected quiz answers or report text as custom analytics events. About Cloudflare Web Analytics.

The optional AI study prompt is available after a practice quiz. It contains questions from your completed quiz, your selected answers, and the site’s explanations. Copying it places text on your clipboard. Nothing is sent to an AI service automatically; the service’s own terms and privacy practices apply if you paste it there.

Manage this browser’s data

Download a backup before deleting if you want a record. Close other quiz tabs after deletion because they can still hold answers in memory. Deleting browser storage does not delete files you previously downloaded or text you sent elsewhere.
